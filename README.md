## 🚀 Project Overview

Employee attrition is one of the most critical challenges faced by organizations. Understanding why employees leave helps businesses improve retention strategies, reduce hiring costs, and increase workforce productivity.

This project performs an end-to-end HR Analytics workflow using **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to uncover the key factors influencing employee attrition.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to:

✅ Analyze employee attrition patterns

✅ Identify factors driving employee turnover

✅ Generate actionable business insights

✅ Build an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard

✅ Support data-driven workforce decisions

## 💡 Key Insights

🔹 Employees working overtime showed significantly higher attrition rates.

🔹 Employees with lower job satisfaction were more likely to leave.

🔹 Certain departments experienced higher employee turnover.

🔹 Lower salary groups exhibited higher attrition.

🔹 Employees with shorter tenure were more likely to resign.
